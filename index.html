OBL Player — Privacy Policy

10 Oct 2026 · @Naj

Summary

OBL Player does not collect, sell or share your personal data with us. Everything you add to the app stays on your own device, apart from the connections the app makes to the servers you choose and to Google Play for purchases.

This policy applies to the OBL Player app (package com.oblplayer.tv) on Android phones, tablets and Android TV / Google TV. OBL Player is a media player: it plays IPTV playlists and TV guides that you supply. It does not provide, host or sell any channels or content.

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are built into the app.
  • Your playlists, favorites and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not receive any of the data below. It is created and kept on your device by the app.

Data Where it lives Why
Playlist URLs, Xtream/server addresses and any username or password you enter On your device only (app storage) To load your channels from your provider
Channel list and TV guide (EPG) downloaded from your provider On your device only To show channels and programs
Favorites, recently watched, last watched channel, playlist names and order On your device only To remember your choices
App settings (start channel, zapping mode, auto-refresh interval) On your device only To personalize the app
Premium status and purchase state Google Play, with a small cache on your device To unlock Premium features

The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and it does not read any of them.

Free trial. The app records the date of its first launch on your device to run a 30-day free trial. This date never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Android backup is turned off for the app. Your playlists and credentials are not copied to cloud backups or to a new device. After a reinstall you add your playlists again; Premium is restored from your Google Play account.

How we use information

The app uses the data above only to work as a player on your device:

  • To download and refresh your playlists and TV guide from the addresses you entered.
  • To play the streams you select.
  • To remember favorites, history and settings.
  • To check whether your Premium purchase is active.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

Third-party services

The app connects to the following services. Each one sees your IP address and the technical details of a normal network request, under its own privacy policy.

Your IPTV provider and EPG source. To load a playlist, guide or stream, the app contacts the server addresses you entered. Your provider may log your IP address, credentials and viewing activity. We have no control over this and are not responsible for the provider's practices. Check your provider's privacy policy.

Google Play Billing. If you buy Premium (annual subscription or lifetime purchase), Google processes the payment and shares only your purchase status with the app. We never see your card or payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Script host (hls.js). To play some HLS streams the app may load the open-source hls.js library from a public content delivery network when it is not bundled in the app. That request exposes your IP address to the host.

OBL Player does not include advertising networks, analytics, crash-reporting or social media SDKs.

Permissions, security and retention

Permissions. The app asks for three Android permissions only: Internet (to reach your servers), Network state (to detect whether you are online) and Google Play Billing (for purchases).

Security. The app blocks navigation to external sites and embedded frames, and its internal bridge accepts messages only from its own content. Because many IPTV servers use plain HTTP, the app allows unencrypted connections; streams and playlists loaded over HTTP are not encrypted in transit. Use HTTPS addresses where your provider offers them. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device locked and protect your provider credentials.

Retention and deletion. Data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete a playlist inside the app, or erase everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling it. We hold no copy and cannot recover it for you.

Children, your rights, changes and contact

Children. OBL Player is a general-purpose player and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Content comes from your own provider, so a parent or guardian should supervise what a child watches.

Your rights. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection law, you keep your rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection. You can exercise them over data on your device directly in the app and in Android settings, and over data held by Google or your IPTV provider by contacting them.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing or release notes.
