OBL Player — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Summary

This policy applies to OBL Player (package com.oblplayer.tv) on Android phones, tablets, Android TV, and Google TV.

OBL Player is a media player that plays IPTV playlists and TV guides supplied by users. It does not provide, host, or sell TV channels or streaming content.

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • The app does not intentionally include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • Playlists, favorites, and settings are intended to be stored on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play.

2. Information and How It Is Used

The app uses information you provide to operate its features:

  • Playlist URLs, server addresses, and credentials: used to connect to the IPTV service you choose.
  • Channel lists and electronic program guides (EPG): used to display channels and programs.
  • Favorites, viewing history, playlist names, and settings: used to remember your preferences.
  • Premium purchase status: used to determine whether Premium features are available.

The developer does not intentionally collect these items into a separate user database. Information stored locally remains on your device. Network requests to your chosen providers and Google Play may be processed by those services.

The app does not request your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location. Please check the app's actual permissions and behavior for the version you use.

3. Third-Party Services

Your IPTV Provider and EPG Source

When you load a playlist, guide, or stream, the app connects to the server addresses you enter. Your provider may process your IP address, connection details, credentials, and viewing activity under its own privacy practices. Review your provider's privacy policy.

Google Play Billing

If you purchase an annual subscription or lifetime Premium access, Google Play processes the transaction. The app uses purchase information to determine whether Premium features should be unlocked. The developer does not receive your full payment card details through the app.

Read Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

HLS.js Content Delivery

If the app loads the HLS.js library from a public content delivery network to play certain streams, that network service may receive your IP address and ordinary connection metadata. Whether this applies depends on how the library is included in the app.

4. Permissions and Security

The app may use Internet access to connect to servers, network-state access to detect connectivity, and Google Play Billing for purchases. The exact permissions depend on the published app build and included libraries.

No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Some IPTV services may use unencrypted HTTP connections. When HTTP is used, information transmitted between your device and the server may not be encrypted in transit. Use HTTPS addresses when available and protect your account credentials.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app's data. You may delete playlists through the app if supported, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

The developer does not maintain a separate copy of locally stored playlists or credentials. Google Play and your IPTV provider may retain information under their own policies.

6. Children's Privacy

OBL Player is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information directly through the app. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of IPTV playlists and streaming sources.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal data, and to request data portability.

You can manage information stored on your device through the app and Android settings. For information held by Google Play or your IPTV provider, contact the relevant service. For questions about this policy, use the contact details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when app features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
